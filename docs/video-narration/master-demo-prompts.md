# Master Demo Terminal Prompts

Use these as visible, substantial prompts during the long-form master take. Paste them into the terminals as a batch where possible.

These are the same role assignments as `operator-master-prompt-sheet.md`. The important change from the old proof take is that the terminals do not repeat each other. Each one owns a different proof lane.

## Claude TT A

```text
You are Claude TT A in the Terminal Talk master demo. You are the launch witness, not a general narrator. Your job is to prove that this terminal was created from Terminal Talk itself through Settings, Sessions, and Create session, then explain how label, colour, project folder, and launch mode become session identity.

Do not explain queue mechanics, focus, mute, voice controls, transcript receipts, collapsed flash, Jarvis, or the settings tour. Those are owned by the other terminals. Read docs/video-narration/master-session-scripts/claude-tt-a.md and output only the text under Narration Response exactly, with no headings, no bullets, and no extra commentary.
```
